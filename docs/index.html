About This Model

A faithful reimplementation of FiveThirtyEight's Elo rating system, verified to match their published probabilities to 4 decimal places on 2010–2020 games. Ratings are updated after every game using a K-factor of 20, home-field advantage of 65 Elo points, and margin-of-victory multiplier. Each season, ratings revert one-third of the way back to 1505. No injuries, no weather, no market data — pure Elo. Picks are committed to GitHub before kickoff and never edited afterward.
